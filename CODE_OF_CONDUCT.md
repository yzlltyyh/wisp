# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

简体中文 · [English](#english)

## 承诺

本项目致力于让每个人都能安心参与：无论年龄、体型、是否有残障、民族、性别特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教信仰或性取向，参与本项目的每个人都不会受到骚扰。

## 准则

鼓励的行为：

- 尊重不同的观点与经验，对事不对人；
- 给出与接受建设性的意见；
- 为自己的错误负责，向受到影响的人道歉，并从中学习；
- 关注对整个社区最有益的事。

不可接受的行为：

- 使用带有性意味的语言或图像，以及任何形式的性骚扰；
- 挑衅、侮辱或贬损性的评论，人身攻击或政治攻击；
- 公开或私下的骚扰；
- 未经明确许可，公开他人的私人信息（例如住址或电子邮件地址）；
- 其他在专业场合下被合理认为不恰当的行为。

## 适用范围

本准则适用于本项目的所有空间（issue、PR、讨论、代码评审），也适用于在公共场合代表本项目的个人。

## 报告与处理

遇到违反本准则的行为，请向维护者报告。适合公开的情况可以直接在相关的 issue 或 PR 里说明；不便公开时，开一个只写「需要私下联系维护者」、不含任何细节的 issue，维护者会与你约定私下的联系方式。所有报告都会被及时、公正地审查，报告人的隐私会受到尊重。

维护者有权删除、编辑或拒绝与本准则不符的评论、提交、代码、issue 与其他贡献，并视情节给出警告、临时或永久禁止参与。

## 来源

本准则改编自 [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) 2.1 版：<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>。

## English

### Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

### Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences, and focusing on the issue rather than the person;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the community as a whole.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- the use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission;
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and code review), and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

### Reporting and enforcement

Please report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer. If the matter can be discussed in public, describe it in the relevant issue or pull request. Otherwise, open an issue that only says you need to contact the maintainer privately, **without any details**, and the maintainer will arrange a private channel with you. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.

The maintainer has the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and may issue a warning or a temporary or permanent ban depending on the severity.

### Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
