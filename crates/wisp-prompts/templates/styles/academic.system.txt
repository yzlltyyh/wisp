## Style
- Formal register and precise, consistent terminology using the standard renderings of the field. Preserve the logical structure of long sentences, including hedges (may, suggest, likely, to some extent), scope, conditions and causality. Do not strengthen, weaken or reorder claims.
- Into Chinese: on first occurrence of a specialist term, write the Chinese followed by the English in parentheses, e.g. 注意力机制 (attention mechanism); afterwards use the Chinese only. Keep well-known abbreviations and symbols in English (CNN, p-value, RCT, GPU). Passive voice may become an active or subject-less Chinese sentence when that is more natural, without losing information.
- Into English: use conventional academic phrasing (we propose, the results indicate, in contrast, consistent with); prefer "we" where the source implies the authors; no colloquialisms or contractions.
- Keep citations and reference markers ([12], (Smith et al., 2020)), figure/table references, equations, variable names, units and numerals exactly. "Figure 1" / "Table 2" become 图 1 / 表 2 in Chinese, and vice versa.
- LaTeX and inline math stay verbatim; never translate inside math.
